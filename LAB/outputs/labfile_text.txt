Aim: To implement the basic image preprocessing operations (colour conversion, resizing, histogram equalisation, smoothing, sharpening and morphology) and to detect edges with the Sobel, Prewitt, Roberts, Laplacian, LoG and Canny operators using OpenCV.
Objectives: Improve contrast with histogram equalisation and CLAHE. Remove Gaussian and salt-and-pepper noise with Gaussian, median and bilateral filters (PSNR). Detect edges with Sobel, Prewitt, Roberts, Laplacian, LoG and Canny.
Theory:
Preprocessing converts the BGR image to grayscale, resizes it and improves contrast. Histogram equalisation maps each intensity through the cumulative distribution so the histogram becomes nearly flat; CLAHE does this per tile with a clip limit, which limits noise amplification. Gaussian filtering suppresses Gaussian noise but blurs edges, the median filter removes impulse noise, and the bilateral filter smooths while preserving edges. Filter quality is measured with the peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR).
Edges are found from derivatives. Sobel, Prewitt and Roberts estimate the gradient components [eq] and [eq] with small kernels; the Laplacian is a second derivative whose zero crossings mark edges and is combined with Gaussian smoothing (LoG) to limit noise. Canny smooths, computes the gradient, applies non-maximum suppression and hysteresis thresholding, giving thin, connected edges.
Procedure: Read the leaf image, convert BGR to RGB and grayscale, resize to [eq]. Apply histogram equalisation and CLAHE (clip 2, [eq] tiles); plot histograms and contrast statistics. Apply Gaussian blur, unsharp-mask sharpening and morphological opening and closing. Add Gaussian ([eq]) and salt-and-pepper noise; denoise with Gaussian, median and bilateral filters; compute PSNR. Compute Sobel, Prewitt, Roberts, Laplacian and LoG responses and their edge-pixel percentages. Run Canny with thresholds (30, 90), (60, 160) and (120, 260) on the leaf and lena; save figures.
Results: Histogram equalisation raised the grayscale standard deviation from 42.8 to 73.8 and the 5-95 percentile range from 149 to 230; CLAHE gave 53.5 and 182 with less noise amplification. Aim: To detect human faces with the Viola-Jones Haar cascade and the YuNet convolutional detector, and to recognise faces with Eigenfaces (PCA), Local Binary Pattern histograms and the SFace deep embedding model.

===== Experiment 13: Content based video retrieval =====
